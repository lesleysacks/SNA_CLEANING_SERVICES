Privacy notice.

How we handle your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

SNA Cleaning Services, No. 06 Millree Court, Lady Grey Street, Paarl, Western Cape, is the responsible party for the personal information described here. Our Information Officer is Andrè Wesley Muller, Managing Director. You can reach him on 073 844 9868 or andremuller093@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote or contact us, we collect what you choose to give us:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your area or suburb and the type of property
  • The service you need, how often, your preferred date and any other details you add

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To arrange, carry out and invoice the cleaning work you book
  • To contact you about that work

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How it reaches us

Our quote form does not store your details on this website. When you press "Send request via WhatsApp", your details are sent to us through WhatsApp, a service run by Meta. If you choose email instead, they are sent through your own email provider. Those services handle your message under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your request, and client records for as long as the law requires for business and tax purposes. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us using it. Contact our Information Officer using the details above.

If you are not happy with how we handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
