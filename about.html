About us

Established in 2009, delivering trusted cleaning excellence.

We have built our reputation on discipline, reliability, and a commitment to leaving every space refreshed and beautifully maintained.

Our story

Cleaning for all business — and your home.

SNA Cleaning Services is a registered company, established in April 2009 by founder and sole shareholder Andrè Wesley Muller. From our base in Paarl, we serve clients across the Cape Winelands and the broader Western Cape.

We are a micro business with 38 trained staff and more than 467 clients served to date. Our goal is simple: to keep town houses, flats, private homes, offices and schools clean, hygienic and healthy, so our clients are satisfied every time.

We believe no two clients' needs are exactly the same. That's why we treat our clients as partners and give special attention to what each of them needs.
