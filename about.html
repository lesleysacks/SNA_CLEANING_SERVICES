About us

Established in 2009, delivering trusted cleaning excellence.

We have built our reputation on discipline, reliability, and a commitment to leaving every space refreshed and beautifully maintained.

Our story

Premium cleaning with a personal touch.

SNA Cleaning Services has served homes and businesses across Paarl and the broader Western Cape since 2009. Our approach is simple: combine professional standards with thoughtful hospitality and meticulous execution.
The founder and sole shareholder of the company is Andrè Wesley Muller. We are a micro business that employs thirty eight (38) staff members currently. The main objective of our company is to make sure that town houses, flats, private houses, offices, schools etc. are clean and tidy to meet the criteria of genuine, hygienic and healthy environment, to ensure our client satisfaction at all times.

From boutique residences to busy commercial offices, we bring organisation, consistency, and a calm presence to every visit.
