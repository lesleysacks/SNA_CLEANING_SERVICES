Dear André,

Thank you for the opportunity to look at the SNA Cleaning Services website. Below is a summary of what's working, what's currently holding the site back, and a three-step plan to turn it into a dependable source of new cleaning contracts.

## What's already working

- A clean, modern design that looks professional on desktop.
- Clear contact details, including WhatsApp, which is how most of your clients prefer to reach you.
- Genuine photos of your team in branded uniforms. These are more convincing than any stock photo.
- A strong story: established in 2009, 38 staff, more than 467 clients, including schools and government departments.

## What we found

A full technical review turned up issues that are quietly costing you enquiries and credibility:

1. **The quote form doesn't send anything.** When someone fills in the form on the Contact page and presses the button, the message goes nowhere. Every one of those potential clients is lost.
2. **The map shows the wrong address.** It points to 70 Lady Grey Street instead of No. 06 Millree Court.
3. **Parts of the site look broken on mobile.** The Contact page slides sideways on phones, only one of your three testimonials can be seen, and some sections stay blank until the visitor scrolls.
4. **Your footer text is invisible.** It's black text on a dark blue background, on every page.
5. **The "Recent work" and Gallery photos are stock images, not your jobs.** Clients who recognise them may question what else on the site isn't real.
6. **Some numbers and testimonials need updating.** "16 years" is now 17, client numbers date from 2022, "100% satisfaction" can't be backed up, and the testimonial names contain typos.
7. **Services don't match your company profile.** Mattress, blinds, car seat, lounge suite, sign & logo cleaning and "undercover" cleaning are missing, and the services list changes from page to page.
8. **Google can't easily find you for local searches.** Searches like "office cleaning Paarl" or "cleaning company Wellington" bring in local work, but the site isn't set up to show up for them. There's no Google Business Profile link and no reviews.

## The plan

### Phase 1 — Fix & Build Trust *(essentials, about 1 week)*

- Make the quote form work, so enquiries reach your email and WhatsApp straight away. Add fields for phone number, area, property type, how often, and preferred date, so you can quote faster.
- Add a "WhatsApp us" / "Call now" button that stays on screen on mobile.
- Fix the map, the footer, the mobile layout issues and the testimonial slider.
- Replace stock photos with real SNA photos: before/after shots from your jobs, your team and your vehicles. We can guide your team on what to photograph, or arrange a short photo session.
- Update your stats and testimonials (with permission from the clients named), and put one consistent services list on every page.
- Make the site load faster on mobile data.

### Phase 2 — Get Found Locally *(about 2 weeks)*

- Set up and optimise your **Google Business Profile**, so you appear on Google Maps with photos, hours and reviews.
- Create individual pages for your main services (office, contract, deep, window, carpet & upholstery, mattress, etc.) and for the areas you serve (Paarl, Wellington, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Cape Winelands).
- Add behind-the-scenes Google markup so search engines understand your business, location and hours.
- Set up a professional email address on your own domain (e.g. info@snacleaning.co.za).
- Add simple visitor tracking, so you can see how many people visit, call, WhatsApp or request a quote.

### Phase 3 — Grow *(ongoing, optional)*

- A **"Commercial & Government Clients"** page with your B-BBEE level, CSD registration, insurance and a downloadable company profile. This is what procurement teams look for when choosing a supplier.
- A simple review routine: a link you send after each job to collect Google reviews.
- A monthly care plan covering updates, new job photos, backups, and a short monthly report on enquiries and visitors.

## What we'll need from you

- Your confirmed list of services and the areas you cover.
- Permission from the clients you'd like to quote as testimonials, and any updated client numbers.
- B-BBEE certificate, CSD number and insurance details (for Phase 3).
- Access to your domain (snacleaning.co.za) and any existing Google Business Profile.
